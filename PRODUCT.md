# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Gamers & Players**: Looking to discover, download, and play studio titles on Google Play Store (Spook-A-Chess, Run Mania, Hop'n Top) and itch.io (Carmine Tangles, Drive Me Home, Neo Mania).
- **Publishers, Investors & Press**: Evaluating the studio's portfolio, production quality, narrative IP (Project Origin / Kaali lore), and trajectory.
- **Enterprise & Creative Clients**: Businesses and brands seeking custom game development, AR/VR experiences, gamification, or mobile app engineering.
- **Talent & Job Applicants**: Game developers, 3D artists, designers, and engineers exploring open roles and studio culture in Nashik, India.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the premier digital hub and flagship brand presence for Elyndor Interactive. The site presents the studio's live portfolio, builds anticipation for upcoming original IP, introduces innovative ventures like STREETPLAY, offers client engineering/XR services, and recruits top-tier indie game development talent.

## Positioning

A grounded, ambitious indie game studio hailing from Nashik, India, creating tactical, arcade, horror, and action-adventure experiences with authentic depth, alongside real-world geo-social gaming apps. Rejecting generic corporate hyperbole in favor of tangible shipped products and distinct craft.

## Operating Context

- Public web accessible globally across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- Integrates with external distribution platforms: Google Play Store, itch.io, YouTube (trailers), Discord (community), LinkedIn, Instagram.
- Hosted and deployed on GitHub Pages (`elyndorinteractive.in`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Live Game Catalog**: Direct links, platform badges, gameplay descriptions, and playable builds.
- **Upcoming Games Pipeline**: Teaser art, trailers, and status indicators for upcoming titles (Hop'n Top, Project Origin, Project Homies, Can You Escape My Brain?).
- **STREETPLAY Showcase**: Dedicated high-energy sub-brand page for the real-world GPS territory gaming app.
- **Service Inquiries**: Functional Web3Forms contact form routing client leads.
- **Careers Hub**: Direct role application workflows via email.
- **Constraints**: Pure, lightweight client-side HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript (with an experimental Next.js alternative in the workspace); must load fast globally, maintain 60fps animations, and remain accessible without plugin bloat.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Elyndor Interactive
- **Voice**: Authentic, human, confident, and direct. Free of AI buzzwords or grandiosity.
- **Visual Identity**: Dark cinematic palette (rich deep blacks, warm gold accents `#d4af37`, terracotta/emerald badges, warm off-white typography `#e8e0d0`).
- **Typography**: Cinzel (classical, prestigious serif for headings) and Inter (crisp, readable sans-serif for body).
- **Core Product Architecture**: Unified studio ecosystem interconnecting Games, STREETPLAY, Services, Team, Careers, and Contact.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live published games: Spook-A-Chess (Play Store), Run Mania (Play Store), Carmine Tangles (itch.io), Drive Me Home (itch.io), Neo Mania (itch.io).
- Shipped game trailers on YouTube (Run Mania, Project Origin Episode 1).
- Official branding assets, vector social icons, original game posters, and screenshots in `assets/`.
- Verified studio presence and active community on Discord, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Instagram.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Hype**: Lead with playable games, real trailers, and authentic engineering—never empty marketing claims.
2. **Unified Ecosystem**: Every product, from mobile casual to tactical battlers and geo-social apps, strengthens the Elyndor studio universe.
3. **Impeccable Performance**: The site must feel silky, fast, and responsive on all devices without layout jank or invasive scripts.
4. **Accessible Authority**: Sophisticated aesthetics paired with intuitive navigation and readable contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG AA contrast standards across all headings, body copy, and UI controls.
- Responsive scaling from 320px mobile screens to 4K ultra-wide monitors.
- Full keyboard and screen-reader navigable links and forms with clear focus indicators.
