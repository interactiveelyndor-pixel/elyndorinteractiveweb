App Interface

Designed for Movement, Competition & Belonging

Every screen of StreetPlay is built with athletic precision, tactile cyber-visuals, and instantaneous real-world geolocation.

01 / Onboarding

Move. Capture. Belong.

Step into the arena. Calibrate your shoes, connect your GPS, and see your neighborhood transformed into an interactive territorial grid.

02 / City Progress

Personal Dashboard

"A fitter city is a brighter city." Monitor your daily streak, overall municipal capture percentage, local routes, and nearby flash challenges.

03 / Active Workout

Live Capture Run

Real-time HUD displaying distance, active pace, and glowing neon path mapping. Enclose polygons on foot to claim new zones from rival clubs.

04 / Squad Wars

Territory & Rivals

Defend your turf across Riverside, Harbor Walk, and East Quay. Challenge rival crews like the NightRunners and top the citywide leaderboards.

Core Pillars

Built Different From Everyday Fitness Apps

StreetPlay isn't just about counting calories—it's about making fitness thrilling, social, and deeply rewarding.

🌐

Real-World GPS Gaming

Walk, jog, or sprint through your actual neighborhood streets to enclose and claim geographic sectors on a shared live canvas.

⚡

Arcade-Grade Motivation

Turn tedious workouts into high-stakes turf conquests. Daily streak shields, localized flash events, and municipal rank boosts.
